Frequently Asked Questions

Does this match the exact token count?

No, it's an estimate, not a byte-for-byte match to a real BPE tokenizer. Roughly 4 characters per token for English; non-Latin scripts use more tokens per character. Check the model's official tokenizer for exact counts.

Why do non-Latin scripts use more tokens than English?

Most AI models train their BPE vocabulary mostly on English text, so a single Korean, Chinese, or Japanese character often splits into multiple byte-level tokens. The same character count uses more tokens than English.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. All calculations happen entirely in your browser and your text is never sent anywhere.
