Frequently Asked Questions

What word list does this use?

A curated list of about 8,000 common English words. Very rare or highly technical terms may not be included.

Can I filter by starting letter?

Yes, use the starting letter filter to only show words beginning with a specific letter.

Is anything I type uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
