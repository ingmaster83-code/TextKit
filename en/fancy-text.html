Frequently Asked Questions

Will these special characters show up in every app?

These are real Unicode characters, not an actual font, so they display correctly in most apps and browsers. Some older devices or fonts may show a box (□) instead.

Does it work on non-Latin text?

Bold, fullwidth, strikethrough, and underline work on any script. Italic and circled styles use Unicode blocks reserved for Latin letters and digits only, so other scripts pass through unchanged.

Is my text stored on a server?

No. All conversion happens entirely in your browser and your input is never sent to a server.
