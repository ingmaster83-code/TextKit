Pull emails, URLs, phone numbers, IPv4 / IPv6 addresses, dates, amounts, hashtags, mentions, colour codes and UUIDs out of logs, mails and documents. Remove duplicates, sort, see where each match first appears (line:column) and get a count summary by type, or supply your own regex. Korean resident registration numbers and card-number formats are reported with a privacy warning.

What this tool does

Pick out just the values you need from long text. Your text is not uploaded.

  • Types: email, URL, Korean and international phone numbers, IPv4, IPv6, dates (ISO and Korean), KRW amounts, numbers, hashtags, @mentions, hex colours, UUIDs, Korean and English words
  • Privacy check: Korean resident registration number formats and Luhn-valid card numbers are found and flagged
  • Tidying: de-duplicate, sort by appearance, alphabetically or by frequency, with counts and first position (line:column)
  • Custom regex: i m s u flags and clear errors for invalid patterns
  • All mode: a per-type total / unique summary table and a copy-friendly one-per-line list

💡 Tips

  • Trailing periods, commas and brackets after a URL are dropped automatically; use a custom regex if you need them.
  • Collect only the IPs from a log and sort by frequency to see the busiest addresses.
  • Paste the result into 'Line Sorter' for more tidying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are phone numbers and emails validated?

They are matched by format only. Whether a number or address is in use is not checked, and similar digit strings can occasionally slip in.

Which regex flavour is used?

The browser's JavaScript regex. Turn on the u flag to use Unicode properties such as \p{L}.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
