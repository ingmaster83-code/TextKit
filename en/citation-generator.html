Frequently Asked Questions

Which citation styles are supported?

Website citations in APA 7th edition, MLA 9th edition, and Chicago (notes-bibliography) format.

What if the page has no listed author?

Leave the author field blank and each style's rules for no-author sources (leading with the site name or title) are applied automatically.

Is my input stored on a server?

No. Everything is generated entirely in your browser and your input is never sent to a server.
