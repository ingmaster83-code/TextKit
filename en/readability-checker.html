💡 Tips

  • One idea per sentence makes a huge difference to readability.
  • Shorter, simpler words raise your Flesch score.
  • Check your blog posts, reports, and emails before you publish them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What formula does this use?

For English text it calculates the standard Flesch Reading Ease score based on average sentence length and syllables per word.

What should I do if my score is low?

Break long sentences into shorter ones and replace difficult words with simpler alternatives. Aim for one idea per sentence.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. All analysis happens locally in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
