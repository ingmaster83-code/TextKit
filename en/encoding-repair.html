What this tool does

Guess and restore the original form of garbled strings. Your text is not uploaded.

  • Mojibake: UTF-8 read as Latin-1 (CP1252), double encoding, UTF-8 read as EUC-KR / Shift_JIS / GBK / Big5 / EUC-JP, and bytes of those sets read as Latin-1
  • Decoding: percent-encoding (URL), HTML entities (&#xC548;), \uC548 and \xE9 escapes, MIME encoded-words (=?UTF-8?B?...?=) and Quoted-Printable
  • Scoring: ranks candidates by replacement characters (�), control characters, typical mojibake patterns and Hangul / Latin mixed words
  • Handles many lines at once and collects the best result per line

Frequently Asked Questions

Is full recovery always possible?

Only while the bytes are intact. Text whose characters became '?' or '�' cannot be restored for those parts.

Are EUC-KR and CP949 the same here?

The browser's euc-kr label behaves as CP949 (extended Wansung), supporting nearly all Hangul syllables, and this tool uses the same rules.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
